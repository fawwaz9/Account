# Calendar request: using UBC's Academic Calendar data

Written for: you, to send to UBC. The message itself is written for UBC's
calendar office, who know nothing about the app. Phase 2 item 8, decisions D2
and D7.

**Why:** UBC's terms of use ([ubc.ca/site/legal](https://www.ubc.ca/site/legal.html))
forbid reproducing the calendar's information "without the prior written
consent of the University of British Columbia". Until they answer, prerequisites
come from syllabi (D6).

**Who to send it to:** the Academic Calendar is published by UBC Enrolment
Services. Take the contact from the calendar sites: Vancouver
([vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca](https://vancouver.calendar.ubc.ca/)) and Okanagan
([okanagan.calendar.ubc.ca](https://okanagan.calendar.ubc.ca/)). One message
covering both is fine if they share an office. I haven't looked up or verified
an address, so check it there.

---

**Subject:** Permission to use Academic Calendar course data in a free student
planning tool

Hello,

I'm a UBC student, and I've built a free, invitation-only planning app used by
about 20 students. It tracks courses, grades and deadlines. I'd like it to show
each course's prerequisites, so students can check what a course requires
before planning a term.

Your terms of use ask for written consent before Calendar information is
reproduced. So I'm writing to ask whether I may store course codes, titles,
credits and prerequisite information from the Vancouver and Okanagan Academic
Calendars. I'd refresh it once a year and show it only to the app's signed-in
users, with a link back to the Calendar as the authoritative source. The app is
non-commercial, and prerequisites would be shown as guidance only, never as a
block.

I'm happy to follow any conditions you'd attach: attribution, how often it's
refreshed, or a different source you'd prefer I use.

Thank you,
<your name>

- **If no:** the catalogue keeps growing from syllabi alone, which the plan
